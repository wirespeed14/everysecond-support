EverySecond

Workout Timer – Support

EverySecond is an interval workout timer for iPad and Mac. It supports AMRAP, EMOM, Tabata, Custom interval, and Stopwatch workouts, with a Get Ready countdown, spoken cues, a two-week schedule, and a history of your workouts and personal records.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue: github.com/wirespeed14/everysecond-support/issues

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Include your device (for example "iPad Air M2") and its iPadOS or macOS version. A GitHub account is needed to post an issue.

Beta testers: you can also use TestFlight's built-in "Send Beta Feedback" option, which lets you attach a screenshot.

Questions

What do I need to run it?
An iPad on iPadOS 27 or later, or an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 27 or later.

Is there an iPhone version?
No. EverySecond is designed for iPad and Mac.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. Your workouts, schedule, history, notes, and settings never leave it, so deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.

Why does my music get quieter?
On iPad, EverySecond briefly lowers other apps' audio around its spoken cues and beeps, then restores it. Macs don't lower other apps' audio.

Does the timer keep going if I switch to another app?
The timer keeps the correct time, but sounds play only while EverySecond is in front. When you return, it catches up.
