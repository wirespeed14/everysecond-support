Privacy Policy

EverySecond Workout Timer · Effective November 18, 2026

In short: EverySecond does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The workouts you create, your schedule, your workout history (including results, mood, and notes), and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your iPad or Mac. EverySecond has no accounts, no servers, and no cloud sync, so none of this information is sent anywhere.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party code or services inside the app.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device deletes all of its data.

TestFlight

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's own privacy policy. The developer receives only what you send through TestFlight or post on the support page.

Children

EverySecond does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the support page.
